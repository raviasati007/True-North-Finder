How to copy coordinates from Google Maps
  1. On a computer, right-click the spot on the map. The coordinates are the first item in the menu; click them to copy.
  2. On a phone, touch and hold the spot to drop a pin. The coordinates show in the search bar or the sheet below; tap them to copy.
  3. You can also paste the full link from the browser address bar. Shortened links such as maps.app.goo.gl hide the coordinates, so open them first.

How to mark true north on the ground
  1. Set a straight stick upright on flat ground, checked with a plumb line.
  2. Mark the tip of its shadow. Mark it again every few minutes, from about 30 minutes before solar noon to 30 minutes after.
  3. The shortest shadow, which falls at solar noon, lies on the true north-south line.
  4. At any other time, draw the shadow line, then rotate it by the angle in the Sun panel to get true north.
